10 · Bottom bar & sheets (notebook look)

The bar as the notebook's cloth or leather band with gold-foil lettering, and a ribbon bookmark that pokes out of the active button (tap the buttons: the ribbon moves and the paper sheet changes). The sheets are paper pages with printed small-caps headings and handwritten chips. Three bar materials, each with its own chip style.
